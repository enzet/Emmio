Emmio is an experimental research project designed to explore new techniques of measuring language ability and lanugage learning.

Emmio uses English Wiktionary data (along with Kaikki, that uses wiktextract library), Tatoeba

Using Emmio you can:

  • Learn new words with spaced repetition, typed recall, and example sentences.
  • Check your vocabulary against a language's most frequent words to estimate your level.
  • Track your progress over time, per language.
